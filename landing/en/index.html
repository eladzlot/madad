What happens to the data?

All processing happens in the patient's browser — not on our server, not in a third-party cloud. The report is created on the patient's own device and stays there. We simply never see the data, because it never passes through us.

What this means in practice: the patient downloads the report and brings it to the session — just as they would fill in a paper form and bring it along. The difference: they filled it in from the sofa, and nobody had to print anything. You don't receive the report directly — the patient is the one who brings it.

Why I built this

Therapy should have outcomes. As a therapist, it matters to me to know that I am actually helping my patients — not just to feel that we are making progress, but to see it happen.

This came into focus while working with a patient with PTSD. The relationship was good, there were insights, and it felt like we were on the right track. But when we looked at the measures — nothing had moved. Only when we changed focus did the change come, fast and clear.

Since then, measurement is not an add-on for me — it is an essential part of good therapy.

Our mission at the Trauma Recovery Center is to advance the highest standard of care. Madad was built so that measurement is simple enough to become a routine part of therapy — not an extra you have to work for. Free, for every therapist.
